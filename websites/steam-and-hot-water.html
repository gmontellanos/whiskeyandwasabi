Case Study

An old-school feel for a heating
contractor who values his privacy.

Steam & Hot Water · Heating Contractor · San Francisco, CA

The Situation

David runs a heating contracting business in San Francisco, and he came in with a clear sense of what he didn't want just as much as what he did. He wanted his privacy respected, no personal photos of himself anywhere on the site, and he wanted every inquiry to come straight to him, through the phone number and email already listed on the page, nothing routed through a third-party form. He also wanted the site to feel simple and old-school, not trendy or over-designed.

What I Built

A straightforward, professional service site with genuine old-school character, clean layout, no unnecessary flash, built to earn trust quickly rather than impress with design tricks. No personal photography anywhere on the site, respecting exactly what he asked for. Every path to contact him leads directly to his own phone number and email, keeping him fully in control of how and when customers reach him.

The Result

A site that feels exactly like David wanted it to, simple, trustworthy, and entirely on his own terms, with customers reaching him directly.
