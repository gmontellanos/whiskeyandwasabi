Case Study

The client who changed how
I build every site since.

Cloudy to Clear · Professional Organizing · SF Bay Area

The Situation

Claudia runs a professional organizing business, and her existing site wasn't giving her what she needed. She specifically wanted the ability to blog and keep her own content fresh without having to call a developer every time she had something new to share.

What I Built

A full site rebuild in clean, custom code, along with something that hadn't existed in my process before: a real content management system, built specifically so Claudia could write and publish her own blog posts whenever she wanted. It's the same CMS I now build into every client site. The build also includes a HoneyBook integration for her contact form, so inquiries flow directly into the client management system she already uses to run her business.

Custom Build Blog-Ready CMS HoneyBook Integration Full Rebuild

A Quick Note on Process

This project taught me a lot, and it's directly responsible for a tool I now bring to nearly every client I work with. Ana Made Studios and an upcoming project for a Western artist are both using the same CMS this build led me to create.

The Result

Claudia has a site that's fully her own to grow, blog posts included, with inquiries landing exactly where her business already runs.
