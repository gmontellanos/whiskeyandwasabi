The Process

Every project starts the same way, with questions that matter. I send a discovery survey before I design a single thing. It covers your brand, your audience, your competitors, and what success looks like for you. That's what makes the final result feel like yours.

① Discovery Survey

Before anything is designed, I send a survey covering your business, audience, aesthetic preferences, and goals. This is what one client called "simple yet poignant questions," it's the foundation everything else is built on.

② Strategy + Planning

Based on your survey answers, I map out the site structure, page goals, and copy direction. Every page has a job, and we define it before the first line of code is written.

③ Design + Build

Custom design, coded from scratch. No templates, no drag-and-drop builders. Clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that loads fast and looks intentional on every screen size.

④ Review + Launch

You review the site, we make refinements, and then we launch. I handle domain connection, Google Analytics setup, and Search Console submission, so your site is findable from day one.

Questions I Hear a Lot

Do I have to pay you every month?

No. It's a one-time investment for the build. The only ongoing cost is your domain renewal, usually $10 to $15 a year, which you pay directly to your domain registrar, not to me.

What if I need to update something later?

Every site includes a simple content management system, so day-to-day things like your phone number, hours, pricing, or blog posts are yours to update anytime, no coding needed, no cost. For anything beyond that, new pages, design changes, or added features, just reach out and I'll quote the work before starting anything.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Your site is built in clean, custom code that you fully own, not hosted inside someone else's platform. That means faster load times, no monthly subscription, and a site that's genuinely yours.

I don't have photos or write-ups ready, can we still start?

Yes. The discovery survey helps figure out what we actually need, and I can guide you through gathering the rest as we go.

Do you work with businesses outside Sacramento?

Yes, this is a fully remote studio. Current and past clients include businesses in New Orleans, the SF Bay Area, Woodland CA, and Sacramento.

What happens after my site launches?

You'll have full access to update your own content through the CMS. For bigger changes down the line, new pages, redesigns, or added features, I'm happy to help, that work is quoted separately from the original build.
