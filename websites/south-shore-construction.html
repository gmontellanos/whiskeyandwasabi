Case Study

A carpenter with no website,
now getting found online.

South Shore Construction · Carpentry & Construction · New Orleans, LA

The Situation

South Shore Construction came to me with zero online presence. No website, nothing for a potential customer to find when searching for a carpenter they could trust. In a trade where word of mouth only goes so far, that's a real problem, especially against competitors who do show up online.

He had a few clear priorities going in: a website that actually matched his existing logo and brand, real photos of his best work, not stock images, and an easy way for potential customers to reach him directly.

What I Built

A clean, trust-forward website built entirely from scratch and designed around his existing logo, so the whole brand feels cohesive rather than like a website bolted onto a business that already had its own identity. The site features his actual completed projects, giving homeowners real proof of his craftsmanship rather than generic stock photography. A built-in contact form makes it simple for a potential customer to reach out directly from the site, no extra steps, no hunting for an email address.

The Result

South Shore now has a professional home online for the first time, one that looks like his actual business, showcases his actual work, and makes it simple for the right customer to reach out.
