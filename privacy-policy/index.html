Privacy Policy

How your information
is handled here.

Last updated September 2026

I want you to feel just as comfortable on this site as I hope you feel working with me. Here's a plain-language rundown of what information gets collected when you visit or reach out, and what happens to it.

What I Collect

When you fill out a form on this site, whether it's a quote request or a content strategy inquiry, I collect whatever you enter: your name, email, phone number, and any project details you share. That's it, nothing more than what you willingly type in.

This site also uses Google Analytics to understand general traffic, things like which pages get visited and roughly where visitors are coming from. This is aggregated, anonymized data, not anything tied to you personally.

How I Use It

Form submissions go straight to my inbox so I can respond to your inquiry. I don't add you to a mailing list, sell your information, or share it with anyone outside of what's needed to run this site. Analytics data helps me understand how the site is doing, nothing more.

Tools This Site Uses

A couple of third-party services help this site function:

EmailJS handles form submissions, delivering what you enter directly to my inbox.

Google Analytics tracks general site traffic and usage patterns.

Both are standard, widely used tools, and neither has access to more than what's described above.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to track site visits. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some site features may not work as smoothly if you do.

Your Choices

If you'd like to know what information I have on file for you, or you'd like it deleted, just reach out at info@whiskeyandwasabi.com. I'm happy to help.

Updates to This Policy

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and adjust the date above.
